About

J Banners writes about people caught between the lives they have made and the lives they might have lived.

His books tend to start with a piece of paper somebody should have thrown away: a shopping list, a land register, a ward register taken at six in the morning, a birth card written down late, an assessment thirty-eight years overdue. He likes institutions, mostly for what they forget, and he follows footnotes further than he should.

He reads across literary fiction, crime, science fiction, dark fantasy, noir and memoir, and is as happy in a book written for twelve-year-olds as in one written for their grandparents. He keeps a list of the best last lines he has read, which he will not show anyone. Away from the page he cooks with more ambition than a weeknight strictly requires.
